New families usually start with a 45-minute assessment, no referral needed.
We don't ask a child to fit the room. We shape the room around the child.

Every child on the spectrum processes sound, touch, movement, and space differently. Our therapists spend the first sessions simply observing — what settles a child, what overwhelms them — before building a plan around it.

Families stay in the room, and stay in the loop. You'll know exactly what we're working on and why, and you'll leave each session with something small you can practice at home.

What we offer

Support built around how your child processes the world.

Each program is adapted to the individual child — these are the areas we most often work on.

Sensory integration therapy

Guided play with movement, texture, and pressure to help a child's nervous system respond to the world with less overwhelm and more comfort.

Speech & communication

Building functional communication — spoken, signed, or picture-based — so your child can express needs, ask for help, and be understood.

Occupational therapy

Getting started

What the first few weeks look like.

A clear, unhurried path from first contact to a plan that's actually theirs.

1

Intake call

A 20-minute conversation about your child, your concerns, and what you're hoping changes.

2

Sensory assessment

A relaxed, play-based session where our therapist observes how your child responds to different input.

3

A plan you review

We walk you through goals and methods in plain language before a single session is booked.

4

Ongoing sessions

Weekly sessions with progress notes, and a plan check-in every six weeks.
